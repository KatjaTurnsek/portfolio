Scope
Individual Project Exam 2
Year
2026
Tools

Outcome
Grade A

The brief

The goal was to turn the Holidaze API into a clear end-to-end booking experience. Customers needed to discover venues, understand availability, and manage bookings, while venue managers needed a separate set of tools for creating and maintaining listings. The interface had to keep these connected journeys understandable without exposing the complexity of the underlying API.

Challenge

Outcome and reflection

The completed project received a Grade A and delivered complete customer and manager journeys in one responsive application.
